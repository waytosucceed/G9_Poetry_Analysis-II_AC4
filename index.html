Writing Exercise:

Construct an 8-16 line poem.
Make sure to use at least 1 symbol (either conventional or contextual)

OR

Answer the following questions (based on the poem you read) in your own words:

  1. What does Lochinvar say happened when he asked to marry the bride in the past?
  2. What does Lochinvar ask to do before he leaves, according to stanza four?
  3. What did the bride do with the goblet, and what did the knight do with it?
  4. What did Lochinvar do before the bride's mother could stop him?
  5. What did Lochinvar say as he took the bride's hand?
  6. What did the bride-maidens whisper to each other?
